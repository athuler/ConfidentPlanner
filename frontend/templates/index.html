About

Data sources

  • London Planning Datahub — applications and decisions (GLA, OGL v3)
  • postcodes.io — postcode → coordinates, borough, ward (OGL v3 / OS OpenData)
  • Historic England — conservation areas (OGL v3)
  • planning.data.gov.uk — flood risk zones (OGL v3)
  • ONS Census 2021 TS006 — population density (OGL v3)
  • OpenStreetMap — parks via Overpass (ODbL); basemap tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors
  • ONS Geoportal — borough boundaries (OGL v3)
